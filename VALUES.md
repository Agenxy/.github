# What we care about

Live and let live. Aim to be elite, and good.

It is about the code at the end of the day. This file exists so we do not lose
our way, not to be recited. [ENGINEERING.md](ENGINEERING.md) is the part you will
actually use day to day.

## The ones that do not bend

- **Apache 2.0**, chosen when the repository is created rather than argued about
  later.
- **We do not log what you do with our software.** No telemetry, no analytics, no
  usage reporting. If our servers were breached tomorrow there would be nothing
  about you in them to find.
- **No trials, keys, DRM or feature gates.** It works, offline, for anyone.
- **Nothing we build depends on a paid or closed service** that somebody else can
  switch off.
- **We cannot change what it does on your machine** after you install it. No
  forced updates, no remote kill switch.

## The rest

Your machine is yours and our software is a guest on it. It does what you asked,
says what it did, and does not decide on your behalf what you are allowed to
want.

We would rather shut a project down than harm or mislead the people using it.
That is the actual decision procedure, not a flourish.

Credit is requested, not policed. If we have got something wrong, tell us and we
will fix it rather than argue. Patent trolls get nothing.
